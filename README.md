A tired dark lord's monologue: Mortimer has trained his apprentice Mortimer for thirty-four years, and now there are two Mortimers.

The joke is the paperwork: the forms are confused, the minions are confused, the tower is confused — and the notes are wrong, but he keeps them anyway.
